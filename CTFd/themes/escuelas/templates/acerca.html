  2. 2026

    CTF en escuelas secundarias 2.0

    {% trans %}Benjamín Freccero Starnari adapted CTFd in his undergraduate thesis «Una plataforma libre y personalizable para “CTF en escuelas secundarias”» (Licenciatura en Sistemas, February 2026): he translated the platform, added the author role for teachers and put it to work in an inter-school CTF with 4 schools and 40 students.{% endtrans %}

  3. 2026

    {{ _("Challenge bank") }}

    {% trans %}Emilia Romero designed 32 challenges in six categories and integrated them into the tool in her thesis «Diseño e integración de contenidos en la herramienta “CTF en escuelas secundarias 2.0”. Aportes a la formación en Informática para jóvenes», together with the bank from which any teacher can put together a competition.{% endtrans %}
